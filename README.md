# CFA Level I, in 30-second cards

Read it at **https://amishah1998.github.io/cfa-l1/**

Every Learning Module of the 2026 CFA Level I syllabus, subject by subject (Economics and Ethics so far), as swipeable cards: a short story, a diagram, the formula in plain words, a worked example, and exam-style questions at the end of every chapter. Each chapter can be read on its own. The Menu searches every card.

Each subject also comes as a PDF, one card per page, with quiz answers on the page after each question.

Works on a phone (swipe up) and on a laptop (wide two-column cards).

Independent study notes, not affiliated with or endorsed by CFA Institute. CFA is a trademark of CFA Institute. People, firms and numbers in the stories are made up.
